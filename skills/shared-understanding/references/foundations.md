# Design basis

This skill adapts four established communication practices. They were not written for coding agents, and citing them does not make this skill scientifically validated or endorsed by their authors.

## Grounding and common ground

Clark and Brennan describe communication as coordinated action built on common ground that changes during the exchange. The practical lesson here is narrow: expose the working interpretation early, and update it when the interpretation changes. Do not assume that silence creates agreement.

Source: Herbert H. Clark and Susan E. Brennan, [“Grounding in Communication”](https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1990s/Clark%2C%20H.H.%20_%20Brennan%2C%20S.E.%20_Grounding%20in%20communication_%201991.pdf) (1991).

## Analytic tradecraft

ICD 203 requires analytic products to distinguish source information from assumptions and judgments, explain consequential uncertainty, state the main message up front, address implications, and explain changes in judgment. This skill maps those distinctions to observation, inference, assumption, and user-visible consequence.

Source: Office of the Director of National Intelligence, [ICD 203: Analytic Standards](https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf).

## Communicating uncertainty

UK Government Analysis Function guidance recommends making critical limitations prominent and explaining how uncertainty changes interpretation or use. This skill therefore puts a material caveat next to the claim it limits, rather than in a final disclaimer.

Source: UK Government Analysis Function, [“Communicating quality, uncertainty and change”](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/communicating-quality-uncertainty-and-change/).

## Plain language

ISO 24495-1 frames plain-language information around relevance, findability, understandability, and usability for its intended readers. This skill applies that orientation by leading with observable behavior, matching the user’s technical depth, and layering detail instead of dumping every internal detail.

Source: International Organization for Standardization, [ISO 24495-1:2023 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/78907.html).
